Most businesses don't get paid the moment they finish the work, and don't pay suppliers the moment a bill arrives. In between, money is *owed*. **Accounts receivable** is the total your customers owe you. **Accounts payable** is the total you owe your suppliers. Keeping an eye on both is how you avoid running out of cash while looking profitable on paper.

<ShortVersion>

- **Receivables**: invoices you've sent that customers haven't paid yet. An asset.
- **Payables**: bills you've received that you haven't paid yet. A liability.
- Sending an invoice adds to receivables; the customer's payment clears it. Bills work the same way in reverse.
- An **aged** report sorts what's owed by how late it is: Current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ days past due.
- The older a debt gets, the less likely it is to be paid. Chase early.

</ShortVersion>

## Two sides of the same idea

<ArApMirror />

Receivables are an asset because the money is yours, just not in your bank yet. Payables are a liability because you've agreed to pay; the cost is already counted, only the payment is still to come.

## Payment terms and due dates

Every invoice and bill has a due date, usually set by **payment terms**: how many days the customer has to pay. dubbl offers **Due on Receipt**, **Net 7**, **Net 15**, **Net 30**, **Net 45**, **Net 60** and **Net 90**. "Net 30" means payment is due 30 days after the invoice date. A customer's own terms (on their contact) win; otherwise your default terms under **Settings › General** are used.

Until the due date, an unpaid invoice is **Current**. The day after, it's overdue, and dubbl marks it **Overdue** on its own.

## Ageing: how late is late?

An aged receivables report puts every unpaid invoice in a bucket by how many days it is past its due date. Each 30 days, an unpaid invoice moves one bucket to the right:

The shape of this report tells you a lot. Most money in **Current** is healthy. A growing pile in **61-90** and **90+** means customers are paying slowly, or not at all, and your cash is stuck with them.

## Good to know

- The Overview's to-do list flags overdue invoices and bills, so you don't need to run the report to know something is late.
- [Payment reminders](/docs/guides/payment-reminders) chase overdue customers by email for you.
- If a customer will never pay, **Write off** on the invoice records the unpaid amount as a bad-debt loss and removes it from what you're owed.
- A [customer statement](/docs/guides/statements) lists everything a customer owes in one document. Useful when they've lost track.
- On the payables side, [paying bills](/docs/guides/paying-bills) in batches helps you pay on time without paying early.
