Every page in dubbl has the same layout. The rail on the far left picks an area of the business, the panel next to it lists the pages in that area, and the top bar holds search, Tilly and a button to create whatever the page is about. Learn these once and every screen feels familiar.

<Shot name="overview" alt="The Overview page with the rail, the panel, search and Ask Tilly marked" pins />

## Get around in four moves

<Steps>

<Step>

Pick an area in the **rail**: Overview, Contacts, Sales, Purchases, Accounting, Tax and the rest. Hover an icon to see its name. Tilly sits at the top, and **Help** and **Settings** at the bottom.

</Step>

<Step>

Pick a page in the **panel**. In Sales, for example, you'll find **Invoices**, **Quotes**, **Cash sales** and **Credit notes**, then **Payments** and **Prepayments** under Getting paid.

</Step>

<Step>

Use the **create button** at the top right to add something new. It changes with the page: **New Invoice** on invoices, **New Quote** on quotes, **New Contact** on contacts.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Search** in the top bar, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows), to jump straight to an invoice, bill, contact or document.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Search looks for invoice and bill numbers and references, contact names, emails and tax numbers, and document file names. Type at least two characters.
- On any list, press <kbd>/</kbd> to jump to its search box.
- Click a row to preview it on the right without leaving the list. Double-click, or choose **Open**, to go to the full page. Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through the previews.
- The tiles at the top of a list are filters too. Click one to show only the records behind that number.
- Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>) to open Tilly from anywhere.

## Lists work the same everywhere

<Shot name="finding-your-way-sales" alt="The invoices list with the organization switcher, the panel, the New Invoice button and the list's search marked" pins />

Invoices, quotes, bills, contacts and most other lists share one layout: totals at the top, a chart you can hide, then status tabs, a search box, filters, **Group** and **View** (to pick columns), and **Export** to download a CSV. Tick rows to act on several at once: the bar that appears at the bottom has actions for that list, plus **Ask Tilly** and **Export**.

## Search

<Shot name="finding-your-way-search" alt="The search box with acme typed and Acme Corp found under Contacts" narrow />

Results are grouped into Invoices, Bills, Contacts and Documents. Pick one with the mouse, or move with the arrow keys and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Tilly, the assistant

**Ask Tilly** in the top bar opens Tilly in a panel beside the page you're on, so you can keep working while you ask. Tilly can read your books, explain a number, draft a record and read PDFs, photos and CSVs you drop in. Changes always wait for your approval before anything is saved.

<Shot name="finding-your-way-tilly" alt="Tilly open in a panel next to the Overview page" />

On a self-hosted server, Tilly needs an AI key before it can answer. Until one is set, the panel says Tilly isn't switched on yet. See [Tilly](/docs/guides/tilly) for everything it can do.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Switching organizations

Click your organization's name at the top of the panel to see your role, the number of members, the base currency and your plan. Other organizations you belong to are listed there too; pick one to switch. **New organization** starts [setup](/docs/guides/setup) for another business.

<Shot name="finding-your-way-org" alt="The organization menu showing Demo Company with members, currency and plan, and New organization" narrow />

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Your account and notifications

The bell in the top bar shows your latest [notifications](/docs/guides/notifications). Your initials at the bottom of the rail open your menu: **Account**, **Plans & billing**, a theme switch and **Sign out**. **Help** opens these docs.

</Fold>
