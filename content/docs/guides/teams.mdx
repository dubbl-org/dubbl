A team is a named group of people in your workspace: Engineering, Design, the warehouse crew. Teams make it quicker to staff projects: assign a team to a project with one role, instead of adding people one by one.

<Shot name="teams" alt="The Engineering team's page with its member count, colour, contacts and the list of team members" pins />

## Create a team

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Teams** and choose **New Team**.

</Step>

<Step>

Give it a **Name**, a **Description** if you like, and pick a **Color**. Choose **Create Team**.

</Step>

<Step>

Open the team and choose **Add Member**. Search for the person and pick them. Repeat for everyone on the team.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="teams-new" alt="The New Team sheet with a name, description and colour" narrow />

## Good to know

- Teams are made of people who sign in to your workspace, not payroll employees. Invite people first in [Members and roles](/docs/guides/members-and-roles).
- One person can be on several teams.
- A team's colour shows on its card and its page, so teams are easy to tell apart.
- Deleting a team doesn't remove anyone from your workspace. It does take people off any project they were on only through that team.

## See who's on which team

**Teams** > **Members** lists everyone in your workspace with a badge for each team they're on. People who aren't on a team show **No teams**. Search by name or email to find someone.

<Shot name="teams-members" alt="The Members page listing a person with an Engineering and a Sales badge" pins />

## Put a team on a project

Open a [project](/docs/guides/projects), go to its **Team** tab and choose **Assign Team**. Pick the **Team** and a **Role for team members**: **Manager**, **Contributor** or **Viewer**. To add one person instead, choose **Add Member**, pick a role, and give them their own **Hourly Rate** if their time is billed differently.

When you assign a team, everyone on it shows up in the project's member list with the role you picked. The team stays in sync with the project:

- Someone who is already on the project keeps their current role.
- People you add to the team later join the project too, and people you take off the team leave it.
- Removing the team from the project (the remove button on its row under **Teams**) takes off the people who were there only through that team. People you added with **Add Member**, or who are also on another assigned team, stay.
- To take one person off the project, use the remove button on their card in the member list.

## Finding and sorting teams

**Teams** shows how many teams and members you have, the average team size and your largest team. Each team card shows the initials of the first few people on it. Search by name, and sort by name or by number of members.

<Shot name="teams-list" alt="The Teams page with the team totals, search and sort, and a card each for the Design, Engineering and Sales teams" pins />

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Editing and deleting a team

On a team's page, **Edit** changes its name, description and colour; choose **Save Changes**. **Delete** removes the team after you confirm. To take someone off a team, use the remove button on their row in **Team Members** and confirm.
