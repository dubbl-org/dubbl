The desktop app puts dubbl in its own window, with a menu bar, keyboard shortcuts and a native look on macOS and Windows. It's the same dubbl you use in the browser: the pages come from dubbl Cloud or your own server, so every new feature reaches the app the moment it reaches the server, without an app update.

## Get started

<Steps>

<Step>

Download the installer for your computer from dubbl's [GitHub releases](https://github.com/dubbl-org/dubbl/releases): a `.dmg` for macOS (Apple silicon or Intel), an installer `.exe` for Windows, or an AppImage or `.deb` for Linux.

</Step>

<Step>

Open the app. On **Welcome to dubbl**, choose **dubbl Cloud**, or **Self-hosted** and type your server's address (like `books.example.com`).

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Continue**. The app checks that a dubbl server answers at that address, then opens its sign-in page.

</Step>

<Step>

Sign in as you would in the browser. Signing in with Google or Apple happens inside the app.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Only one copy of the app runs at a time. Opening it again brings the existing window forward.
- The app remembers its window size and position, and the server you chose.
- Links to other websites open in your normal browser. PDFs and previews from dubbl open in a separate app window.
- Packaged apps check for updates on their own and install them when you quit.
- If the server can't be reached you'll see **Can't reach dubbl**. The app keeps trying; choose **Try again** to retry now or **Change server** to connect somewhere else.

## Menus and shortcuts

- **Go** jumps to **Overview** (**⌘1**), **Sales** (**⌘2**), **Purchases** (**⌘3**), **Contacts** (**⌘4**), **Accounting** (**⌘5**), **Reports** (**⌘6**), **Tilly** (**⌘J**), **Notifications** and **Documents**.
- **View** has **Back** (**⌘[**), **Forward** (**⌘]**) and **Reload** (**⌘R**).
- **Settings…** (**⌘,**) opens dubbl's settings.
- **Help > dubbl Documentation** opens these docs in your browser.

On Windows and Linux, use **Ctrl** instead of **⌘**.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Switch to another server

Choose **Change Server…** in the app menu (on Windows and Linux, the **File** menu), pick **dubbl Cloud** or **Self-hosted**, and choose **Continue**. The app checks the new address before it switches, and you sign in again there.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Build it yourself

The app lives in the `desktop/` folder of the dubbl repository. With Node.js and pnpm installed:

```bash
cd desktop
pnpm install
pnpm dist:mac     # dmg and zip, Apple silicon and Intel
pnpm dist:win     # Windows installer
pnpm dist:linux   # AppImage and deb
```

See [Contributing](/docs/developers/contributing) for working on the code.
