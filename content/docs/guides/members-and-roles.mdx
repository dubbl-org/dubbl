Everyone who works in your books is a member of your organization. Each member has a role: **Owner**, **Admin** or **Member**. For finer control, give someone a ready-made role like **Read-only**, or build your own, such as a bookkeeper who can enter bills but not touch settings.

## Invite someone

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Settings > Members** and choose **Invite Member**.

</Step>

<Step>

Enter their **Email** and pick a **Role**: **Member** or **Admin**. You can give them a more specific role after they join.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Send Invitation**. They get an email with a link to join, and appear under **Pending Invitations** until they accept.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="members-and-roles-invite" alt="The Invite Member drawer with an email address and the Member role" narrow />

## Good to know

- Admins can invite, change roles, remove people and edit the organization's settings. Only the owner can manage billing, lock past dates or delete the organization.
- The owner can't be removed and their role can't be changed.
- Invitations expire after 7 days. Revoke one from **Pending Invitations** if you sent it to the wrong address.
- The Free plan has one seat. On paid plans every seat is included; see [Billing](/docs/guides/billing).
- A ready-made or custom role replaces the person's base role. A member with **Read-only** can only look, even though members can normally add records.

## Who can do what

<RolePermissionMatrix />

The three base roles in plain words:

- **Owner**: full access. Manages billing, members and all settings. Can't be removed.
- **Admin**: everything except billing, locking dates and deleting the organization. Admins can edit the organization's details on **Settings > General**.
- **Member**: views and creates records (invoices, bills, expenses, contacts), but can't finalize journal entries, approve, or manage members or settings.

## Give someone a ready-made or custom role

On **Settings > Members**, open the menu on the person's row and choose **Assign:** followed by the role. **Remove custom role** puts them back on their base role. The four ready-made roles are:

- **Read-only**: sees data, pay slips, payroll reports and the activity history. Can't change anything.
- **Invoice-only**: invoices, credit notes, contacts and payments, nothing else.
- **Standard**: everyday work (transactions, invoices, bills, expenses, payments, purchases, projects, timesheets and time off) without finalizing, approving or settings.
- **Advisor**: every permission. Meant for your accountant.

Ready-made roles can't be edited or deleted.

</Fold>

## Create your own role

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Settings > Roles** and choose **Create Role**.

</Step>

<Step>

Give it a **Name** and a short **Description** so others know what it's for.

</Step>

<Step>

Tick the **Permissions** it should have. They're grouped by area (Accounting, Invoicing, Bills, Banking and so on); the box next to a group ticks everything in it. Every permission in dubbl is on the list, including **Manage approval workflows**, **Approve purchases**, **Manage integrations & email sending** and **Edit organization settings**.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Create**, then assign the role to people from **Settings > Members**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Invite links

Instead of inviting people one by one, choose **Create Link** under **Invite Links**. Pick the **Default Role** new people get, and optionally a **Max Uses** and **Expires In (days)**; leave them blank for unlimited uses and no expiry. Copy the link and share it. You can turn a link off and on again, or delete it.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Change or remove a member

Open the menu on the person's row:

- **Make admin** or **Make member** switches their base role.
- **Remove** takes them out of the organization. Their past work stays in your books and in the [audit log](/docs/guides/audit-log).

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Accountants and advisors

**Settings > Advisors** lets you invite an accountant, auditor, tax advisor or bookkeeper by email. Once they accept, they can work in your books with the same rights as a member, plus two more:

- They can lock past dates, like the owner.
- They're held only to the stricter **Advisor lock date (optional)** on [Settings > General](/docs/guides/organization#lock-past-dates), when you set one. That lets them post adjustments into a period your team can no longer change.

Advisors are listed on their own page, not under **Members**, so you can't give them a custom role. If your accountant needs more, such as approving or finalizing entries, invite them from **Settings > Members** instead and give them the **Advisor** role.

</Fold>
