A recurring journal is a journal entry on a schedule. If the same adjustment goes into your books every month, such as rent paid by standing order, a monthly management fee or an owner's regular drawings, set it up once and dubbl posts it on each date. Each entry it makes is a normal posted journal entry you can open, check and reverse.

## Set up a recurring journal

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Accounting**, then **Recurring journals**, and choose **New recurring journal**.

</Step>

<Step>

Under **Template Details**, give it a **Name** and choose the **Frequency**: **Weekly**, **Fortnightly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Semi-Annual** or **Annual**.

</Step>

<Step>

Set the **Start Date** (the first entry is posted on this day) and, if it should stop, an **End Date (optional)**. Add a **Reference** if you like.

</Step>

<Step>

Under **Journal lines**, add the accounts and amounts, exactly as you would for a [journal entry](/docs/guides/journal-entries). Debits must equal credits.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Create recurring journal**. It's **On** straight away.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="recurring-journal-new" alt="The New recurring journal drawer: monthly office rent of $3,500, debit Rent Expense and credit Checking Account" narrow />

## Good to know

- Entries are posted straight to your books, not saved as drafts. Check the lines carefully before you create it.
- dubbl posts what's due every night. If the nightly run missed dates while the journal was **On**, it catches up and posts one entry for each.
- A date that falls in a locked period is skipped, and the schedule moves on to the next date.
- Each line needs either a debit or a credit, not both.
- Deleting a recurring journal stops future entries. Entries it already posted stay in your books.

## Post due entries now

You don't have to wait for the nightly run. **Post what's due** on the list, or **Post entries due now** on a recurring journal's own page, posts every entry whose date has arrived. The **Due this week** total shows how many are coming up, including any whose date has passed.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Pause, resume or delete

- **Pause** stops a recurring journal from posting. Choose **Resume** to turn it back on. Dates that passed while it was paused are skipped, not filled in: it picks up at the next scheduled date from today. If the end date passed while it was paused, it shows as **Finished** instead. To pause or resume several at once, tick them in the list.
- **Delete** removes the schedule for good. Past entries are kept.

A journal that reaches its end date shows as **Finished**.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Find your way around the list

Each row shows **How often** it runs, the amount of **Each entry**, the same as a **Per month** figure, the **Next entry** and **Last entry** dates, and how many entries it has **Posted** so far. The totals at the top add up what's posting automatically and what it comes to each month.

</Fold>
