Notifications tell you when something needs a look: a customer paid, an invoice went overdue, something is waiting for your approval, a bank balance dropped below the level you set. They collect in your inbox, behind the bell in the top bar, and you decide which ones also arrive by email.

<Shot name="notifications" alt="The notifications inbox with the Unread filter, categories, search and the Preferences button" pins />

## Choose what reaches you

<Steps>

<Step>

Open the bell in the top bar and choose **View all notifications**, then choose **Preferences**. **Settings > Notifications** opens the same panel.

</Step>

<Step>

For each kind of notification, switch **Inbox** and **Email** on or off. Changes save as you go.

</Step>

<Step>

Under **Email timing**, pick how often emails come: **Right away**, **Every 30 min** or **Every 2 hours**. Anything that happens in between is bundled into one email.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="notifications-preferences" alt="The Notification preferences drawer with Inbox and Email switches for each kind of notification" narrow />

## Good to know

- Every kind starts with **Inbox** on and **Email** off.
- Preferences are yours alone. Each person in the workspace sets their own.
- You aren't notified about things you did yourself. If you record a payment or assign yourself a task, the notification goes to the others it concerns.
- Anything waiting for your approval also shows at the top of the bell under **Waiting for you to approve**, whatever your preferences.
- Notification emails come from dubbl itself, not from your own [mail server](/docs/guides/email).

## Work through your inbox

- **Unread** shows only what you haven't read. The categories (**Invoices & payments**, **Approvals**, **Banking & budgets**, **Integrations** and more) narrow the list.
- **Search notifications** finds one by its text.
- **Mark all as read** clears the unread count.
- The keyboard is quickest: **j** and **k** move, **Enter** opens, **e** marks as read, **x** selects and **#** removes.

Opening a notification takes you to the record or page it's about.

</Fold>

## What you're notified about

| Notification | When it's sent | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invoice overdue** | The night an invoice passes its due date unpaid. Once per invoice. | Owners, admins and whoever created the invoice |
| **Payment received** | A customer payment is recorded against an invoice: by hand, by matching a bank transaction, or when the customer pays online. | Owners, admins and whoever created the invoice |
| **Approval needed** | Something is sent for approval, and again each time it moves on to the next approver. | The approver for that step |
| **Task assigned** | Someone gives you a project task, when it's created or reassigned. | The person assigned |
| **Low bank balance** | Once a day while an account's balance is below its alert amount. | Owners and admins |
| **Budget exceeded** | The day an account's spending in a budget period reaches the budget's alert level. Once per account and period. | Owners and admins |
| **Payroll due** | A draft pay run (or one waiting for approval) has a pay period ending within 3 days, or already ended. Once per pay run. | Owners and admins |
| **Low stock** | An item's quantity on hand is at or below its reorder point. At most once a week per item. | Owners and admins |
| **Stripe payment failed** | A Stripe charge or invoice payment fails. | Whoever connected Stripe |
| **Sync events failed** | Stripe events still fail after every retry. | Whoever connected Stripe |
| **System** | A scheduled backup, a scheduled report or a payment reminder couldn't be sent. At most once a day for the same problem. | Owners and admins |

The checks for overdue invoices, bank balances, budgets, pay runs and stock run once a day, overnight.

## Set the alert levels

**Low bank balance** and **Budget exceeded** only fire once you've said where the line is.

<Steps>

<Step>

For a bank account, open it under **Accounting > Banking**, go to **Settings** and enter an amount under **Low balance alert**. Leave it empty to turn the alert off.

<Shot name="bank-low-balance-alert" alt="The Low balance alert field on a bank account's Settings tab" />

</Step>

<Step>

For a budget, open it under **Accounting > Budgets**, go to **Settings** and set **Spending alert** to a percent of each period's budget, for example 90 to hear about it before the money runs out. New budgets start at 100. Leave it empty to turn the alert off.

<Shot name="budget-spending-alert" alt="The Spending alert field on a budget's Settings tab" />

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Save changes**.

</Step>

</Steps>

[Tilly](/docs/guides/tilly) can set these for you too ("warn me when Checking drops below $5,000").
