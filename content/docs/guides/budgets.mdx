A budget is your plan in numbers: how much you expect to spend on rent, wages or marketing, or to earn from sales, over a year or any other stretch of time. dubbl compares the plan with what's actually in your books, account by account and period by period, and warns you before something runs over.

## Create a budget

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Accounting**, then **Budgets**, and choose **New Budget**.

</Step>

<Step>

Give it a **Budget Name**, a **Start Date** and **End Date**, and choose the **Period Type**: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Yearly** or **Custom**.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Add Line** for each account you want to plan, pick the account and enter the **Annual** amount. dubbl splits it evenly and shows how much that is per period.

</Step>

<Step>

If some periods need more than others (a big campaign in spring, say), choose **Customize period amounts** under the line and set each one.

</Step>

<Step>

Check the **Total budget** at the bottom, then choose **Create Budget**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Actual figures come from posted entries in your books between the budget's start and end dates. Drafts don't count.
- Budgets work for income too. For expense accounts, actual means what you spent; for revenue accounts, what you earned.
- A bar turns amber once an account has used more than 80% of its budget, and red when it's over.
- Changing a budget doesn't change your books. It only changes what the real numbers are compared against.
- **Deactivate** hides a budget from reports and dashboards without deleting it.

## Read a budget

Open a budget to see the whole picture.

<Shot name="budget" alt="A budget's overview: total budget, actual spend, variance, burn rate, days remaining, utilization and the spending trend chart" pins />

- **Total Budget**, **Actual Spend** and **Variance** (what's left, or how much you're over).
- **Burn Rate** projects where you'll end up if you keep spending at today's pace: what you've spent so far, divided by the days gone, times the days in the budget.
- **Days Remaining** and **Utilization** show how far through the budget you are in time and in money.
- **Spending Trend** charts actual against budgeted, period by period.

Below the chart, each account has its own bar. Open **Periods** on a line for the **Period Breakdown**: **Budgeted**, **Actual** and **Variance** for every period.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Change, deactivate or delete

Open the budget and choose the **Settings** tab. You can change the name, dates, period type and lines there, and the **Spending alert**: the percent of a period's budget at which owners and admins get a **Budget exceeded** [notification](/docs/guides/notifications) (100 unless you change it; leave it empty to turn it off). Under **Danger zone**:

- **Deactivate** hides the budget from reports and dashboards. **Reactivate** brings it back.
- **Delete** removes the budget and all its lines for good.

</Fold>
