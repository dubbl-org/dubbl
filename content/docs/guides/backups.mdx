dubbl takes a snapshot of your organization's data every day and keeps it for as long as your plan allows. You can also make one yourself before a big change, download a copy to keep off dubbl, or upload one you saved earlier. Backups are under **Settings > Backups**.

<Shot name="backups" alt="The Backups page with Download Snapshot, Upload Backup and Create Backup" pins />

## Make a backup before a big change

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Settings > Backups**.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Create Backup**, then **Create backup** to confirm. It appears in the list as a manual backup, with its size, how many records it holds and when it expires.

</Step>

<Step>

To keep a copy outside dubbl, choose **Download** on its row, or **Download Snapshot** at the top for a fresh copy of everything right now. Both are a JSON file.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="backups-create" alt="The Create backup confirmation" narrow />

## Good to know

- Automatic backups run about once a day. How long they're kept depends on your plan: 7, 30, 90 or 365 days. See [Billing](/docs/guides/billing).
- Admins and the owner can see, create and download backups. Only the owner can restore, upload or delete one.
- A backup holds your records, not your files. Attachments and documents stay in Documents.
- Restoring replaces your current data, but dubbl saves a snapshot of it first, so you can go back.
- A restore is all or nothing. If any record can't be put back, it stops with a message naming that record, and nothing is changed.

## Restore a backup

Choose **Restore** on the backup's row. The warning explains that this replaces all current data, and that a snapshot of your current data is saved first. Confirm, and every record in the left-hand list above is put back as it was in the backup, lines included. Records of those kinds that you added after the backup was made move to [Trash](/docs/guides/trash), so you can still bring one back.

For a single deleted record, [Trash](/docs/guides/trash) is quicker.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Upload a backup

**Upload Backup** takes a `.json` file you downloaded from dubbl earlier and adds it to the list as an uploaded backup. You can then restore it like any other. Only the owner can upload.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Delete a backup

**Delete** on a backup's row moves it to [Trash](/docs/guides/trash), where it stays for 30 days in case you change your mind. Manual backups count toward your plan's limit of 3, 10, 25 or 50 until they're deleted.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Running your own server?

These backups cover one organization's records. If you host dubbl yourself, back up the database and file storage too. See [Self-hosting: backups](/docs/self-hosting/backups).

</Fold>
