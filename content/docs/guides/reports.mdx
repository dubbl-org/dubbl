Every report in dubbl is built from the same ledger your invoices, bills and bank transactions post to, so the numbers always agree with each other and are always up to date. There's nothing to run overnight: open a report, pick the dates, and read it. Each one is named after the question it answers, like **Money in vs money out**, with the accounting name underneath for your accountant.

<Shot name="reports" alt="The Reports page with report cards grouped by topic and the side panel listing every report" pins />

## Run a report

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Reports** in the sidebar. Reports are grouped by what you want to know: **The big picture**, **Profit & spending**, **Getting paid & paying**, **Tax time**, **Planning ahead**, **For your accountant**, and **Save, schedule & reconcile**.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose a card. Not sure which? **Quick health check** puts this period's key numbers next to the period before.

</Step>

<Step>

Set the dates. Pick **This Month**, **Last Month**, **This Quarter**, **Year to Date**, **This Year**, **Last Year**, or **Custom** for any range. Snapshot reports like **What you own and owe** take a single **As of:** date instead.

</Step>

<Step>

Where you see **Count income and costs**, choose **When invoiced or billed** or **When money moves**.

</Step>

<Step>

Read the report. Click an account name, where it's a link, to see every transaction behind it.

</Step>

<Step>

Take it with you: **Download PDF**, **Export CSV**, or the whole set in **Download a report bundle**.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Reports read posted entries only. Drafts, and anything still waiting for approval, don't count until they post.
- Most reports open on January 1 to today, shown as **Year to Date**. The highlighted button always matches the dates the report is using.
- **Export CSV**, **Download PDF** and the spreadsheet downloads all write normal amounts (so $12.50 is written as 12.50).
- **Export CSV** is greyed out when the report has no rows for the dates you picked.
- You only see reports for the company you're working in. To add several companies together, use [Consolidation](/docs/guides/consolidation).

## Counting on invoice date or when money moves

The basis switch changes when income and costs count, not what's in your books. **When invoiced or billed** (accrual) counts a sale on the invoice date and a cost on the bill date, paid or not. **When money moves** (cash) counts an invoice or bill when it's paid, and a part payment counts its share: if a customer pays half an invoice in March and half in April, half the sale (and half its tax) lands in each month. Bank transactions you categorize count on their own date.

<ReportBasisTimeline />

It's on **Money in vs money out**, **Quick health check**, **Profit by team or project**, **Download a report bundle**, **Sales tax / VAT / GST return** and the Schedule C worksheet. Use **When invoiced or billed** unless your accountant asks otherwise.

## Comparing periods

There are a few ways to see a change, depending on what you're comparing:

- **Quick health check** puts each number next to the period right before the one you picked, with the change in dollars and percent.
- **Compare two periods side by side** lays out earnings, costs and profit for 3 to 12 months, quarters or years in a row, with the change between each.
- **What you own and owe** and **Account balances check** have **Compare to an earlier date**, which adds a second column for any date you pick.
- **Budget vs what actually happened** compares against your plan instead of the past.

## Exporting and sharing

- **Download PDF** is on **Money in vs money out**, **What you own and owe**, **Quick health check**, **Sales by customer** and **Sales by item**. The sales reports also have **Spreadsheet**.
- **Export CSV** is on the list-style reports, such as the aged reports, the general ledger, the trial balance and the 1099 totals.
- **Download a report bundle** puts **Money in vs money out**, **What you own and owe**, **Account balances check** and **Where your cash went** for one period into one spreadsheet, one sheet each. Choose **Download the bundle (spreadsheet)**.

<Shot name="reports-pack" alt="What's in the bundle: four reports and the download button" />

Downloads use the company you're working in, even if you belong to several.

To send reports on a schedule, or to build your own from invoices, contacts, inventory, bank transactions and more, see [Custom reports](/docs/guides/custom-reports).

</Fold>

## From a number to the transactions behind it

Reports are summaries of your ledger, so you can always dig down. On **What you own and owe**, **Account balances check** and **Profit by team or project**, click an account to open **Every transaction, by account** at that account. From a snapshot report it opens from the first day of your financial year to the snapshot date, starting from the account's opening balance, so the last running balance matches the figure you clicked. From there, every line shows its entry number and reference, so you can find the invoice, bill or bank transaction that caused it.
