When a customer pays an invoice, you record the payment on that invoice. dubbl moves the money from what they owe into your bank account and keeps a list of every payment, which invoices it covered and anything left over. Payments made online through a payment link are recorded for you.

<Shot name="payments-received" alt="The payments list with money received this month, most used method, not matched yet, and each payment's matched status and leftover" pins />

## Record a payment

<Steps>

<Step>

Open the invoice the customer paid, from **Sales › Invoices**.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Record Payment**.

</Step>

<Step>

Enter the **Amount** and the **Date** the money arrived, pick the **Method** (Bank Transfer, Cash, Check, Card or Other), and under **Paid into** the bank account it went into. Leave it on **Default bank account** if you only have one.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Record Payment**. The invoice becomes **Paid**, or **Part paid** if there's still something owed.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="invoice-payment" alt="The Record Payment dialog with amount, date and method" narrow />

## Good to know

- A payment can't be more than the invoice still owes. Record the right amount, or see the options below for money paid in advance.
- Pick the bank account the money really went into. That's what lets the payment match the line on your bank feed when you [reconcile](/docs/guides/reconciliation).
- You can't record a payment dated in a period you've [closed or locked](/docs/guides/periods-and-closing).
- Customer credit, from a credit note, a prepayment or an earlier overpayment, is applied from the invoice's **Available credit** box instead. Using credit isn't money received, so it doesn't appear in the payments list.
- Paying several invoices with one payment is possible through the [API](/docs/developers/api-reference). In the app you record a payment on each invoice.

## The payments list

**Sales › Payments** lists every payment from customers. The tiles show what came in **this month** and **last month**, the **Average payment**, the **Most used method** and anything **Not matched yet**. Each payment shows whether it's **Matched**, **Part matched** or **Not matched** to invoices, how much was put toward invoices and any **Leftover**. Turn on the **Came in via** column to tell payments made online (**Online (Stripe)**) from the ones you **Recorded by hand**.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## A single payment

Open a payment to see the **Payment amount**, the bank account it went **Into**, how much was **Put toward invoices**, what's **Not yet used**, and the **Invoices this payment covers**, each linking to its invoice.

If part of a payment is **Not yet used**, choose **Put leftover toward an invoice**, pick one of the customer's open invoices and the **Amount**, and choose **Put toward the invoice**. This uses the money you already recorded: it doesn't add a new payment, and nothing new goes through your bank or your books, because the whole payment was counted when it came in.

## Getting paid online

If you've connected Stripe, invoices can carry a payment link. Customers pay by card from the email or from their [portal](/docs/guides/customer-portal) with **Pay now**, and the payment is recorded against the invoice for you. See [Integrations](/docs/guides/integrations) to connect it.
