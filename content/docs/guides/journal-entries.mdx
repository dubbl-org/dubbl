A journal entry is one movement of money between accounts: some accounts are debited, others are credited, and the two sides always add up to the same total. Most entries are made for you when you send an invoice, approve a bill or categorize a bank line. You only type one in yourself for things nothing else covers, such as money the owner puts in, a correction, or an end-of-month adjustment.

## Add an entry by hand

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Accounting**, then **Transactions**, and choose **New Entry** in the top bar.

</Step>

<Step>

Set the **Date** the entry belongs to and a **Description** you'll recognise later. Add a **Reference (optional)** if there's a document number to match it to.

</Step>

<Step>

Under **Entry lines**, pick an **Account** for each line and put the amount in **Debit** or **Credit**. Use **Add line** for more than two lines, and a **Line memo** if a line needs its own note.

</Step>

<Step>

When both columns add up to the same total, choose **Create entry**. It's saved as a **Draft** and opens on its own page.

</Step>

<Step>

Check it, then choose **Finalize**. The entry is now **Posted** and counts in your reports.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="journal-entry-new" alt="The New manual entry drawer: the owner adds $5,000, debit Checking Account and credit Owner's Equity" narrow />

## Good to know

- **Create entry** stays greyed out until debits equal credits. Under the lines you'll see something like "Debits (5000.00) must equal credits (4500.00)". Every entry needs at least two lines.
- A draft doesn't count anywhere. Your reports only change once the entry is posted.
- Posted entries can't be edited. To fix one, reverse it and enter it again.
- If an [approval workflow](/docs/guides/approvals) covers journal entries, a matching draft can't be finalized until it's approved. Choose **Submit for approval** on the entry; **Finalize** works once every approver has said yes.
- You can't add, post or reverse an entry dated on or before your [lock date](/docs/guides/periods-and-closing), or inside a closed financial year.
- The **Source** column tells you where an entry came from: **Typed in by hand**, or made for you by **Invoices**, **Bills**, **Payments**, **Banking**, **Fixed assets**, **Loans** and so on. Filter by it to see only your own adjustments.

## What gets recorded in your books

The entry you type is exactly what's recorded. Say you put $5,000 of your own money into the business account:

## Check an entry from the list

Click any row to open it in a side panel. **What moved where** lists every line with its account, debit and credit, and the panel shows the date, source, reference, when it was posted and whether it's an original or a reversal. Use the arrows at the top to step through the list, or **Open** for the full page.

<Shot name="journal-entry-preview" alt="A loan payment opened in the side panel: principal and interest debited, Checking Account credited, its source and the Reverse entry button" pins narrow />

From the panel a draft has **Post to books**, **Edit draft** and **Delete**. A posted entry has **Reverse entry**. To post several drafts at once, tick them in the list and choose **Post to books**.

</Fold>

## Undo a posted entry

Posted entries are never deleted or changed. Instead, dubbl posts a matching opposite entry on the same date, so the two cancel out and your reports stop counting it.

<Steps>

<Step>

Open the entry and choose **Reverse entry** (on the entry's own page, the button is **Cancel**).

</Step>

<Step>

Say why. The reason is required and is kept on the entry.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Reverse entry** (or **Cancel entry**). The original is marked **Reversed**, and a new entry called "Reversal of entry #11" plus your reason appears in the list.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="journal-entry-reverse" alt="The Reverse entry dialog asking for a reason" narrow />

An entry can only be reversed once, and only if its date isn't locked. If the mistake was the date, reverse it and enter it again on the right date.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Entries that undo themselves

Some adjustments are only meant to last a month. A classic one is an accrued expense: at month end you record a cost you've had but haven't been billed for, and on the first of the next month it should come off again so the real bill isn't counted twice.

An entry can carry an **Undoes itself on** date. Once it's posted and that date arrives, dubbl posts the opposite entry for you, dated that day. You can set this date when you ask [Tilly](/docs/guides/tilly) to make the entry, or through the [API](/docs/developers/api-reference); the **New Entry** drawer doesn't have the field. The list's **Undoes itself** filter and the **Will undo themselves** total show which entries are waiting.

## Entries in another currency

Entries made in the app are in your base currency. Through Tilly or the API, each line can carry its own currency and exchange rate; dubbl converts every line and checks that the entry balances in your base currency. More in [Multi-currency](/docs/guides/multi-currency).
