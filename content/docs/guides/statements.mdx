A statement lists every invoice, payment and credit note with a customer over a period, with a running balance, so they can see exactly how you got to what they owe. Send one when a customer asks where they stand, or to everyone at month end to nudge the late payers.

<Shot name="statements" alt="The customer statements page with what's to collect, overdue amounts, the period picker and the customer and supplier switch" pins />

## Send a statement

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Contacts › Statements**. Each customer with a balance is listed with what they owe, how much is **Overdue** and their **Last payment**.

</Step>

<Step>

Pick the period at the top: **Last 12 months**, **This year**, **Last 3 months**, **Last year** or **All time**.

</Step>

<Step>

Click a customer to preview their statement, then choose **Email statement** to send it, or **Download PDF** to keep a copy.

</Step>

<Step>

To send several at once, tick them in the list and use the bar at the bottom. The email button counts only customers with an email, for example **Email 3 statements**. **Download PDFs** gets them all.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="statements-preview" alt="The statement preview for TechStart Inc showing 8,000.00 overdue, how late it is and the statement lines" narrow />

## Good to know

- **Email statement** sends straight away to the contact's email, using your [email settings](/docs/guides/email). Contacts without an email are skipped, and the **Can be emailed** tile shows how many have one.
- Statements by email are for customers. Switch to **Suppliers** to see and download what you owe each supplier.
- Credit notes count as payments on a statement, because they reduce what's owed.
- Only invoices and bills that are in your books are on a statement. Drafts, cancelled ones and anything waiting for approval are left out, and so is their part of the **Starting balance**.
- The **Over 90 days** tile is the money most at risk. Start there.

## A statement for any dates

For a period the list doesn't offer, open the contact and go to their **Statement** tab, or choose **Full statement** from the preview. Pick a period such as **This Quarter** or **Year to Date**, or set your own **From** and **To** dates. The tiles show the **Starting balance**, **Total charged**, **Total paid** and **Ending balance**, and every line shows the running **Balance**. Use **PDF** to download it or **Email** to send it.

## Reading the statements list

The tiles total what's **To collect**, what's **Overdue**, the share that's **Over 90 days**, how many customers **Can be emailed**, any **Credit on account** and the **Statement period** you're looking at. In the list, **Oldest overdue** tells you how long the oldest unpaid invoice has been late, and **Open invoices** how many are still unpaid. The preview's **How late it is** bar splits the balance into not due, 1 to 30 days, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90 days late.

</Fold>
