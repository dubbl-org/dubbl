Aged reports sort everything that's still unpaid by how late it is. **Who owes you (and how late)** lists your customers' unpaid invoices (aged receivables). **What you owe (and when it's due)** lists your unpaid supplier bills (aged payables). Both use the same buckets, so you can see at a glance where money is stuck.

<Shot name="aged-receivables" alt="Who owes you (and how late), with the as-of date, a total for each aging bucket and the list of unpaid invoices" pins />

## See who owes you

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Reports** and choose **Who owes you (and how late)** under **Getting paid & paying**.

</Step>

<Step>

Leave **As of date:** on today, or pick an earlier date to see what was owed back then.

</Step>

<Step>

Read the five buckets: **Current**, **1-30 days**, **31-60 days**, **61-90 days** and **90+ days**. Each shows the total and how many invoices are in it.

</Step>

<Step>

Check **Total owed to you**, then the list underneath: **Invoice**, **Customer**, **Due Date**, **Days Overdue** and **Amount Due** for every open invoice.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Export CSV** to work through the list, or to share it with whoever chases payments.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Lateness is counted from each invoice's due date, not the date you sent it. **Current** means not due yet.
- Only what's still unpaid counts. A half-paid invoice shows the half that's left. Drafts and voided invoices are left out.
- Picking an earlier **As of date:** rebuilds the past: invoices issued after that date drop out, and payments received after it are ignored. Handy for a year-end list for your accountant.
- **Total owed to you** should match **Accounts Receivable** on your [balance sheet](/docs/guides/balance-sheet) for the same date. If it doesn't, look for a payment or journal posted straight to that account instead of against an invoice.
- **Export CSV** writes amounts in dollars and cents (12.50), ready to add up in a spreadsheet.

## Reading the buckets

<AgingBuckets />

The further right the money sits, the less likely it is to arrive on its own. Anything in **61-90 days** or **90+ days** usually needs a phone call, not another email. [Payment reminders](/docs/guides/payment-reminders) can nudge the earlier buckets for you.

## What you owe

**What you owe (and when it's due)** works the same way for bills. It has the same **As of date:**, the same five buckets, **Total you owe**, and a list of **Bill**, **Supplier**, **Due Date**, **Days Overdue** and **Amount Due**.

<Shot name="aged-payables" alt="What you owe (and when it's due), with the aging buckets and the total you owe" />

Use it to plan payments: pay what's overdue first, and look at **Current** to see what's coming. When you're ready, see [Paying bills](/docs/guides/paying-bills).

<Fold>

## Acting on the list

- **A customer is in 90+ days:** open the invoice, check it was sent to the right person, and send a reminder or a [statement](/docs/guides/statements) with everything they owe.
- **You'll never be paid:** write the invoice off with a [credit note](/docs/guides/credit-notes) so it stops counting as money owed to you.
- **The customer already paid:** record the payment against the invoice. See [Payments received](/docs/guides/payments-received).
- **Patterns over time:** **How fast you get paid** in Reports shows how many days customers usually take, and how long you take to pay suppliers.
