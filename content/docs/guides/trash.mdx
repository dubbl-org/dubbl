When you delete a record in dubbl it isn't gone straight away. It moves to the Trash and stays there for 30 days, so a slip of the finger is easy to undo. After 30 days it's removed for good. You'll find it under **Settings > Trash**.

<Shot name="trash" alt="The Trash page with a deleted contact and a deleted quote, each with Restore and Delete permanently buttons" pins />

## Bring something back

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Settings > Trash**.

</Step>

<Step>

Find the record: type part of its name in **Search deleted items...**, or narrow the list with **All types**.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Restore** (the circular arrow) on its row. It's back where it was.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- Everything in the Trash is removed for good 30 days after it was deleted.
- You can only restore or permanently delete things you're allowed to manage. For example, restoring an invoice needs permission to manage invoices.
- **Empty Trash** removes everything at once and can't be undone. Only the owner can use it.
- Deleted backups land here too, and only the owner can restore them.
- Files deleted in Documents have their own Trash on the Documents page.

## What ends up in the Trash

Contacts, invoices, bills, accounts, journal entries, products, expenses, quotes, credit notes, debit notes, purchase orders, bank accounts, payments, recurring templates, projects, CRM deals, fixed assets, budgets, loans, documents, document templates and backups.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Delete something for good

Choose **Delete permanently** (the cross) on a row and confirm. It's gone straight away and can't be brought back from the Trash. Restores, permanent deletions and emptying the Trash are all recorded in the [audit log](/docs/guides/audit-log).

</Fold>
