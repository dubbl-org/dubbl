Your organization is the business you keep books for. When you create one, a short setup asks where you're based, what kind of business you run and whether you charge tax. From those answers dubbl picks your currency, builds a chart of accounts for your country and adds the usual tax rates, so you can send an invoice the same day.

<Shot name="setup-tax" alt="The Tax and financial year step of setup, pre-filled for the United Kingdom with VAT, invoice basis and an April year start" narrow />

## Create your organization

<Steps>

<Step>

Sign up, or open the organization switcher at the top of the panel and choose **New organization**. Setup opens.

</Step>

<Step>

Type your business name under **Organization Name** and choose **Next**.

</Step>

<Step>

Pick your country under **Where is your business based?** Each country shows the currency it brings with it. This sets your base currency and which business types you can choose.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose your business type: sole trader, partnership, company and so on, as they're called in your country.

</Step>

<Step>

On **Tax and financial year**, say whether you charge tax (**Yes** or **Not yet**), add your tax number if you have one, and check when your **Financial year** starts. dubbl fills these in from your country. In countries with a cash scheme you also choose **Invoice basis** or **Cash basis**.

</Step>

<Step>

**Industry** and **Referral** are optional, so **Skip** them if you like. Check the summary on **You're all set** and choose **Complete Setup**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="setup-review" alt="The review step showing organization, country, currency, business type, VAT, year end and the UK chart of accounts that will be created" narrow />

## Good to know

- Nothing is saved until you choose **Complete Setup**. You can go **Back** and change any answer before that.
- Your base currency comes from your country. It can be changed in settings until you record your first transaction, and then it's fixed.
- If you choose **Not yet** for tax, the standard rates are still added, but new invoices default to no tax. Turn tax on later when you register.
- The financial year start only sets your reporting year. It doesn't lock anything; [closing a period](/docs/guides/periods-and-closing) does that.
- You can run several organizations from one sign-in. Each one has its own books, members and settings.

## What setup does for you

<SetupPath />

The chart of accounts is only created for a new organization with no accounts and nothing posted yet, so finishing setup again never overwrites real books. The review step tells you which chart you'll get and how many accounts it has.

<Fold>

## Change your details later

Everything from setup lives in **Settings › General**: your name, the **Country (sets your tax rules)**, business structure, **Tax ID / VAT**, address and contact details. Under **Financials** you'll find the **Default currency**, **Fiscal year start** and default **Payment terms**, which new invoices use when a customer has no terms of their own. Choose **Save changes** when you're done.

<Shot name="setup-settings" alt="The Financials section of Settings, General with default currency, fiscal year start and payment terms" pins />

More on these settings, including locking past dates, is in [Organization settings](/docs/guides/organization).

</Fold>

## Bring in what you had before

If the business was trading before you started with dubbl, record where things stood on day one: money in the bank, what customers owed you, what you owed suppliers. Go to **Accounting › Opening balances** and choose **Set opening balances**. [Opening balances](/docs/guides/opening-balances) walks through it.

Moving from another app? [Import your data](/docs/guides/import-data) brings in your accounts, contacts, products and more from a CSV file.

</Fold>

<Fold>

## The getting started checklist

Until you dismiss it, the Overview page shows a **Getting started** card with four next steps: **Add a bank account**, **Set opening balances**, **Set up tax** and **Create your first invoice**. Each one takes you to the right page. Tackle them in any order, then choose **Dismiss / All done**.

</Fold>
