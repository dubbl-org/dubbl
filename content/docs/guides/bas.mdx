If your business is in Australia, you report GST to the ATO on a Business Activity Statement. dubbl works out the GST fields for any period from the invoices, bills and transactions already in your books: the GST you charged (1A), the GST you can claim back (1B), and the difference.

## Prepare your BAS

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Tax** in the sidebar and choose **BAS**. The tab shows for companies based in Australia.

</Step>

<Step>

Pick the period at the top. For a quarterly BAS, choose **This Quarter** or set **Custom** dates for the quarter you're lodging.

</Step>

<Step>

Under **Count GST**, choose **When you record it** (invoice basis) or **When money moves** (cash basis, which most small businesses use). It starts on the basis your company is set up with.

</Step>

<Step>

Compare **GST on Sales (1A)** and **GST on Purchases (1B)**. The big figure underneath is **Amount Payable to ATO**, or **Refund Due from ATO** when you paid more GST than you charged.

</Step>

<Step>

Copy the fields under **GST Information** into your BAS on the ATO's online services or your agent's software.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Export CSV** or **Print** to keep a copy with your records.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Good to know

- dubbl works out the figures; it doesn't lodge with the ATO. Lodge yourself, then mark the period submitted under **Tax Periods**.
- 1A and 1B come from your GST accounts in the ledger, so GST recorded any way (invoices, bills, categorized bank transactions, manual journals) is included. Only posted entries count.
- On **When money moves**, an invoice or bill counts in the period it's paid. A part payment counts its share of the GST and the G figures on the payment date.
- G1 is sales including GST, from invoices and cash sales minus credit notes, in your company's currency. Drafts and voided documents are left out.
- **G3** is sales lines with no GST that aren't exports, such as GST-free items or a customer set to **Don't charge tax**.
- **G10** is bill lines coded to a fixed asset account, including GST. **G11** is the rest of your purchases, including GST.
- The figures show in your company's currency. **Export CSV** writes plain amounts (1250.00).

## Lodging and paying

BAS periods are tracked the same way as any other return. Under **Tax** then **Tax Periods**, add the quarter with **New Tax Period**. After you lodge, choose **Mark submitted** to freeze the G fields, 1A, 1B and NET and move the GST out of the two GST accounts into **VAT / GST Suspense**. When the money moves, open the period and choose **Record payment / refund**.

The full walk-through, with the entries dubbl records, is in [VAT return](/docs/guides/vat-return#tax-periods-filing-and-paying).

</Fold>

<Fold>

## Checking the figures

- **GST on Sales (1A)** looks low? Check the invoices are posted (not drafts) and dated inside the period, and that their lines use a GST rate.
- **Export sales** only counts invoices to contacts with a **Tax number / VAT** and a billing country outside Australia.
- To see the GST on your invoices and bills rate by rate, open **Reports**, then **Tax collected vs tax paid**.

</Fold>
