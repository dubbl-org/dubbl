If you keep stock in more than one place, say a main warehouse and a shop, add each place as a location. A transfer records stock leaving one location and arriving at another, so you always know where your units are, not just how many you have.

<Shot name="warehouses" alt="The Locations page with an overview of active locations, the default warehouse, and a card for each location" pins />

## Add a location

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Inventory**, then **Locations**, and choose **New Warehouse**.

</Step>

<Step>

Give it a **Name** and a short **Code**, such as `WH-WEST`. Codes are unique.

</Step>

<Step>

Add the **Address** if you like, and choose **Create Warehouse**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="warehouses-new" alt="The New Warehouse drawer with a name, code and address" narrow />

## Move stock between locations

<Steps>

<Step>

Open **Transfers** and choose **New Transfer**.

</Step>

<Step>

Pick where the stock comes **From** and where it goes **To**.

</Step>

<Step>

Add each item with how many units you're moving. **Add Item** adds another line.

</Step>

<Step>

Choose **Create Transfer**. It's saved as a **Draft**: nothing has moved yet. The two locations must be different.

</Step>

<Step>

When the stock leaves, open the transfer from the list and choose **Mark on the way**. The units come out of the first location.

</Step>

<Step>

When it arrives, choose **Complete**. The units land in the second location. You can also complete a draft straight away if the move is instant.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Shot name="warehouses-transfer-new" alt="The New Transfer drawer moving 40 shipping boxes from the main warehouse to the east distribution center" narrow />

## Good to know

- Creating a transfer doesn't move anything. Stock leaves the first location when you choose **Mark on the way** (or **Complete**), and lands in the second on **Complete**.
- dubbl checks the first location has enough of every item before anything leaves. If it doesn't, the transfer stays as it was and you're told which item is short.
- A transfer only changes where stock is. Your total units and their value stay the same, so nothing is recorded in your books.
- The value shown on a transfer is the units at each item's current average cost.
- An item's page shows **Stock by location** once it has stock recorded in specific locations.
- **Set Default** marks the location you use most, and only one location can be the default. New items' opening stock and imported stock go there.

## The transfers list

**Transfers** shows what's on the way, what hasn't been sent and what has arrived, the value moved, your busiest route and how long transfers take to arrive. **Top routes** lists the paths stock moves along most; click one to filter the list.

<Shot name="warehouses-transfers" alt="The Transfers list with units on the way, transfers not sent yet, a chart by month, the top routes and a draft, an on-the-way and an arrived transfer" pins />

Click a row to preview a transfer: the route, the items and units, and **Mark on the way**, **Complete** or **Cancel** while it's still open.

## Editing and removing locations

Each location card has **Edit** (name, code, address and **Active**); cards other than the default also have **Delete**. An inactive location can't be the default or receive transfers. You can only delete a location that holds no stock and has no open transfers, so move its stock out first. Use the **All**, **Active** and **Inactive** tabs and the search box to find a location when you have many.

</Fold>
